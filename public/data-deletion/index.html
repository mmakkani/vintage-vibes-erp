Option 1: Remove App Access via Facebook Settings

  1. Open your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.

  3. Find Vintage Vibes Live in the active list and click Remove.
  4. Confirm removal to revoke all Meta access tokens immediately.
