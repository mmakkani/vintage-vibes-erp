1. No Returns Policy (All Sales Final)

Vintage Vibes LLC SPC specializes in unique, one-of-a-kind vintage and thrift clothing inspected, steamed, and photographed prior to listing. Due to the 1-of-1 nature of our inventory, we do not accept returns for any online store or live-claim purchase.

2. No Exchanges Policy

Because each vintage garment is a single unique piece with no duplicate stock, we do not offer exchanges for size, fit, color, or change of mind. Full front/back photography and exact measurements (pit-to-pit chest and garment length in inches, where available) are provided on every product listing so customers can verify sizing before ordering.

3. Pre-Purchase Inspection & Live Questions

  • Customers are encouraged to review all product photos, condition grades, and measurements on vintagevibesgk.com before checkout.
  • If you have any questions about measurements, fabric, or condition prior to completing your purchase, please contact our team before placing your order.

4. Order Issues Before Dispatch

If an error occurs prior to courier dispatch (for example, an unavailable 1-of-1 SKU), our Al Ain fulfillment team will notify you promptly at your registered phone number or email.
