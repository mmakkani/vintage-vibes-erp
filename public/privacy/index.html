Vintage Vibes LLC SPC ("Vintage Vibes", "we", "our", or "us") operates the live retail platform and interactive live-auction broadcast application Vintage Vibes Live at https://vintagevibesgk.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, interact with our live video broadcasts, or authenticate via Meta (Facebook & Instagram) or Google/YouTube services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Public display name, user/page identifiers, handle, and contact details provided when connecting a social channel or placing an order/claim.
  • Live Interaction Data: Public comments, item claim messages (such as SKU claims during live broadcasts), and viewer counts on connected live streams across Facebook Live, Instagram Live, YouTube Live, and our Real-Time Live Room (https://vintagevibesgk.com/live).
  • Technical & Diagnostic Logs: Basic connection logs, IP/browser user-agent headers, and stream telemetry required to keep live broadcasts stable and secure.

2. How We Use Meta (Facebook / Instagram) & Platform Data

Authorized access tokens and permissions granted by page/account administrators (including publish_video, pages_show_list, and pages_read_user_content) are used strictly to:

  • Create and broadcast live video sales sessions from Vintage Vibes studios to connected Facebook profiles/pages and social channels.
  • Read incoming live viewer comments in real time to fulfill customer SKU claim requests during live broadcasts.
  • Reply to viewer inquiries from our studio host console.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer Meta platform data or personal information to third-party data brokers or external advertisers.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Access tokens, order records, and live chat metadata are stored in encrypted/access-controlled databases hosted by Vintage Vibes LLC SPC with strict role-based access control (RBAC) and HTTPS/TLS transport encryption. We retain live broadcast chat logs and order data only as long as necessary to fulfill orders, resolve customer support inquiries, and comply with applicable UAE commercial recordkeeping laws.

4. Cookies & Essential Storage

We use essential browser storage and session cookies strictly for studio authentication, cart/claim reservation, and real-time live room streaming stability.

5. Your Rights & User Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your data at any time. Full self-service and email instructions are published on our User Data Deletion Instructions page.
